## P3. Why we use it

We use enquiry information to understand your request, contact you, prepare a response or quotation, and keep an appropriate record of the enquiry. We use technical records to operate and secure the website, diagnose faults and investigate misuse.

We use optional analytics to measure visits and CTA engagement and improve the website. Under our proposed consent model, optional analytics runs only after you choose “Accept analytics”. Rejecting analytics does not prevent you from browsing or contacting us. We do not put enquiry messages, email addresses or telephone numbers into analytics events, or use analytics to create advertising profiles.

Where relevant, our basis for processing is taking steps you request towards a contract, performing a contract, complying with a legal obligation, pursuing a legitimate interest subject to your rights, or your consent. We use consent for optional analytics under this policy. We do not treat your use of the website as blanket consent to unrelated processing.

## P4. Enquiries are not marketing subscriptions

We use collected email addresses for enquiries, not newsletters or promotional mailing lists. Submitting a form or accepting analytics does not subscribe you to marketing. Replies and reasonable follow-up about the enquiry you made are part of handling that enquiry. If we introduce marketing subscriptions later, we will provide a separate notice and obtain any consent required before using your details for that purpose.

## P5. Your choices and required fields

You choose whether to submit an enquiry. Our online form requires your name, email address, phone number and service interest; these are website form requirements, not a statement that the law requires you to provide them. Without enough contact information, we may be unable to respond or prepare a useful quotation. Business name and message are optional. You can also use the contact methods on our contact page.

Optional analytics is off until accepted. You can change that choice through “Cookie settings” at any time. Withdrawing consent stops future optional analytics processing; it does not make earlier lawful processing unlawful. See P9 for choices about existing records.
