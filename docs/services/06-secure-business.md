# 06. Secure Business (powered by Cloudflare)

Source: `6. Secure Business.pdf` (4 pages).

## In one sentence

Cloudflare's zero-trust security, set up and managed for small and medium businesses, so staff can
reach business systems safely from anywhere without the cost and hassle of a traditional VPN.

## Key idea

The brochure presents security as part of the connectivity service. In the source, Cloudflare
security comes standard with the provider's connectivity. The platform combines:

- **Zero Trust Network Access (ZTNA)**: access decided separately for each user and device.
- **Web Application Firewall (WAF)**: protection for websites and web apps.
- **DNS security**: fast, filtered, encrypted DNS.

## Why it's attractive

Cloud-based secure access, secure remote and hybrid work, no VPN hardware, quick deployment and
onboarding, managed support and monitoring, scaling with the business, and clear policies and
reporting.

## Who it's for

SMEs, hybrid and remote teams, professional services firms, retail and multi-branch businesses, and
any team that needs secure remote access.

## What's in the package (three areas, two modules each)

### 1. Access without exposure

- **Secure remote access**: identity-based access to business apps without exposing the network.
  Access from anywhere, multi-factor authentication, identity-based rules, no public RDP or open
  ports.
- **VPN replacement**: cloud-managed access instead of VPN appliances. Less hardware, faster remote
  connections, less IT complexity.
- Works with Microsoft 365 and Google Workspace sign-in.

### 2. Connectivity and DNS

- **Cloudflare Tunnel / Mesh**: encrypted connectivity between users, devices and internal services.
  Private access to internal systems, device-aware policies, better browsing performance, always-on
  protection.
- **DNS security and performance**: filters malicious domains. Malware and phishing blocking,
  encrypted DNS over HTTPS/TLS (DoH/DoT).
- Protects users at the office, at home or on the road.

### 3. Web app protection

- **WAF for websites and business apps**: DDoS mitigation, bot protection, SQL-injection prevention,
  web attack filtering. (The brochure mislabels this card "Secure Remote Access"; it is the web
  application firewall.)
- **Policies and reporting**: managed support and monitoring, policy creation, onboarding and
  reporting so controls are visible and consistent.
- Protects portals, web apps and customer-facing sites globally.
