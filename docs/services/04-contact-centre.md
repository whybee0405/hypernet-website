# 04. Omni-channel Contact Centre

Source: `4. Omni Channel Contact Centre.pdf` (5 pages).

## In one sentence

One cloud workspace where a team handles every customer conversation (calls, WhatsApp, email,
live chat and social media) with the customer's history on screen and managers watching service
levels live.

## The problem it answers

Customers choose how they contact a business. When calls sit in one system, WhatsApp on someone's
personal phone and email in a shared inbox, customers wait, repeat themselves and get inconsistent
answers. The brochure argues that customers judge a business on how each interaction made them
feel.

## What's in the platform

**Channels**

| Channel | What it does |
|---|---|
| Voice | Professional call handling: smart routing, IVR menus, call recording, live reporting. |
| WhatsApp | Talk to customers on the app they already use, with every chat visible to the team. |
| Email | Shared, organised inbox where each enquiry is tracked to resolution. |
| Live chat | Help website visitors immediately and turn questions into sales. |
| Social media | Reply to messages from several social platforms without leaving the workspace. |

**Workspace and management**

- **Built-in CRM**: the agent sees customer history, so nobody has to repeat themselves.
- **Workflows**: pass work between colleagues and departments inside the platform.
- **Real-time insights**: queues, agent performance and service levels as they happen.
- **Cloud-based**: agents can work in the office, from home or across branches.
- **Scales**: from a handful of support staff to a large centre.

**AI tools**

- **Live translation**: detects and translates 100+ languages so agents can reply in the customer's
  own language.
- **Smart auto-replies**: drafts personalised replies by checking the knowledge base, orders and
  previous tickets.

**Integration**

- Connects to existing e-commerce, retail and CRM systems (Salesforce, Shopify, Office 365 named)
  quickly.
- Fully customisable, with an open API.

## Pricing model (the "package")

There is one product at one price per user:

- **No licensing tiers**: every feature is available to every user; no add-on matrix.
- **Continuous updates**: new functionality ships weekly at no extra cost.

No actual price is given.

## The service wrapper

The brochure stresses that the platform is only half of it. The provider learns how the business
talks to customers first, then designs, deploys, trains staff and supports it. "Technology is only
valuable when people know how to use it."

What great looks like, per the brochure: customers never repeat themselves, every enquiry reaches
the right person, everything is visible in one place, the team replies faster and with confidence.
