# 09. Integration Suite (powered by Flowgear)

Source: `9. Integration Suite.pdf` (3 pages).

## In one sentence

A managed integration service built on the Flowgear iPaaS (integration platform as a service) that
gets a business's systems (CRM, ERP, e-commerce, telephony, monitoring) sharing data automatically
instead of being re-typed by people.

## The platform

- **Low-code**: .NET-based, no-code to low-code, so integrations are built quickly.
- **Visual designer**: workflows drawn visually.
- **Cloud and on-premises**: both managed from one interface.
- **Serious data handling**: multi-endpoint integrations with ETL (extract, transform, load) and data
  mapping.
- **250+ ready-made connectors**: SaaS and on-prem, including Salesforce, HubSpot, SAP and AWS.

Recognition: the brochure shows Flowgear rated 4.5/5 on Gartner Peer Insights for enterprise iPaaS
(January 2025), level with Jitterbit, SAP and Boomi and above MuleSoft and Informatica. Not used on
the site as it is a vendor rating, not a Hypernet one.

## Solutions by use case ("if your systems could talk, what would they fix first?")

| Use case | What it does | Examples |
|---|---|---|
| Hosted VoIP integration | Sync CRM and back-end so phone services provision and report themselves. | Auto-provision lines when an order is placed; pull live jitter and uptime stats via API. |
| Master data management | One source of truth for customer and product data. | Sync customer records across CRM, ERP and support; govern product codes and regional mappings. |
| IoT and network monitoring | Real-time anomaly detection from telemetry. | Ingest from IoT hubs via MQTT or REST; process data as it arrives. |
| IP management | Keep IP address data consistent. | Direct database connectors; watch and sync exported datasets automatically. |

## Getting started: proof of concept

- **No obligation**: a custom proof of concept is built around the customer's actual systems.
- **No risk**: no credit card needed to start.
