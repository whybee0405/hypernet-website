# Hypernet

register: brand

## Product purpose

Marketing site for Hypernet, a South African provider of business internet, VoIP, unified
communications, contact centre software, managed security and system integration. The site's job is
to explain eight services clearly and get a business owner to call or send a message.

## Users

Owners, office managers and IT leads at South African businesses, from single sites to multi-branch operations: a hardware shop in
Tembisa, an accounting practice in Stellenbosch, a dispatch office in Germiston, an online store in
Durban. They are not IT specialists. They arrive after an outage, a bad call, or a security scare,
usually on a laptop at work or a phone between tasks, and they want to know quickly whether Hypernet
can fix their specific problem and who they would be dealing with.

## Brand

- Tagline: "Connectivity with a human voice."
- Personality: direct, dependable, established. A national provider with a responsive service desk.
- Palette: Hypernet Blue (#155EEF) for structure and actions, Coral (#E8703A) for the human accent.
- Visual language: "Night Signal". A dark field with generated glass-and-titanium service renders
  lit by a coral filament, paired with documentary-style scenes of South African teams and customers
  at work (generated, in `public/brand/people/`). No posed headset call-centre stock.

## Tone

Plain, specific, calm and established. Present Hypernet as an organisation with a service desk, engineering, field and account teams, never as a one-person operation. Headings are labels. No sales language, no staged contrasts, no em dashes.

## Anti-references

- Generic telecom imagery: globes, wifi bars, headset call-centre stock, glowing cables as decoration.
- Enterprise SaaS templates: three equal feature cards, hero metrics, gradient text, purple glow.
- Editorial-magazine affectation: serif italic display words, mono eyebrows above every section.

## Strategic principles

- Show the service, then the team that runs it. Every section should answer "what do I get" or "who
  supports it".
- Publish only verified figures.
- One primary action everywhere: Contact us.
